Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Pulse Messenger (“Pulse”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Pulse is operated by Jeremiah Ferrer.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are stored in hashed form; we cannot read them.
  • Content you send: messages, images, videos, audio and other files you send in channels and private chats, with their time stamps.
  • Chat status: whether a private message was delivered and seen, and when.
  • Your connections: friend requests, friends, message requests, people you have blocked, and private nicknames you set for others (only you can see your nicknames).
  • Settings: theme, text size, sound and default mood.
  • Reports: reports you file about other users, including the reason, your notes and a copy of the reported message.
  • Technical data: a sign-in session token kept in your browser storage so you stay logged in. Our hosting and service providers may also log technical data such as IP address and browser type.

We do not use advertising, tracking cookies or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your information.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account and provide chat, friends, requests, file sharing and notifications of new messages while the app is open.
  • To keep Pulse safe: handling blocks and reports, preventing spam and abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To fix problems and improve the service.
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

3. Who can see your information

  • Private chats can be read only by the two people in the chat. Files in a private chat can be opened only by those two people.
  • Channel messages and files (#general, #random, #ideas) can be seen by every signed-in user.
  • Your username can be found by other signed-in users through search.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted. Messages are protected in transit (HTTPS) and by access rules in the database, but they are stored on our servers in readable form. The operator and our database provider have technical access to stored data. We will only look at private messages when needed to handle a report, keep the service secure, or meet a legal duty.
  • The person you message can copy, screenshot or share what you send, so only send what you are comfortable with.

4. Service providers

We rely on: Supabase (account sign-in, database, file storage and live updates), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and jsDelivr (delivery of a code library the app needs). They process data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your information while your account exists. Messages and files stay until the account is deleted. Reports are kept as long as needed to keep the service safe; if a reporter or reported person deletes their account, their name is removed from the report but the report and message copy can be kept.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in Pulse go to Settings, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, messages, chats, friends, settings, blocks, nicknames and uploaded files. Copies held by the people you chatted with (for example screenshots) are outside our control.
  • Block and report: you can block anyone and report any message from inside the app.
  • Access or correction: contact us and we will help, as allowed by applicable data protection laws.

7. Children

Pulse is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, per-user access rules in the database and private file storage. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make important changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
